Abstract

I work on scientific machine learning, building high precision machine learning models that can rival and surpass classical numerical methods. On 1 December 2026 I join CuspAI as Digital Twins Lead. At Google DeepMind I worked on the discovery of unstable singularities in the equations of fluid dynamics and on AI weather prediction. Before that I worked on equivariance, normalizing flows, uncertainty quantification, combinatorial optimization, and medical imaging.
